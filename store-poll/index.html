Swag Store · Agent poll

Before we stock the store, ask the people who'll shop in it.

Five questions. Two minutes at Tuesday's meeting, one text for everyone else. The answers decide what goes on the shelves, not us.

At the meeting

Read these out. Write down what they say.

Say it the way you said it to us: "I'm building a rewards store. You earn credits for closings and for finishing the checklist. I want to know what you'd actually spend them on."

1

If you had credits to spend, what would you want?

Pick three. Hands up, or just shout them out.

Hunter of Homes apparel, hatsClosing gifts for your clientsSigns, business cards, marketingDinner out, movie ticketsGift cardsCE course or license feesHeadshots, brandingOpen house kitSomething else
2

One thing under $50 you'd actually work toward.

This tells you what the small, frequent rewards should be. Expect the newer agents to answer this one.

3

One thing over $250 you'd grind for.

This is the big award. Expect Dave and the producers to answer this one, and expect it to be about their clients, not themselves.

4

Should credits come from showing up, or from closing?

Meetings and check-ins, or deals only. Both is an answer. Their split tells you how to weight the points.

5

Where do you want to see your balance?

Email after each closingTextIn the portalAt the Tuesday meeting

If they say "the portal," that's the first reason they'll have to log in.

The one rule

Don't promise anything in the room. "I'm asking, not committing." It's your money and you haven't priced it yet.

Suggestion box

Last thing you say: "If I didn't list it, tell me after or text me." A couple of them will, and those are usually the best ideas.

For everyone who wasn't there

One text. Three answers.

Send it Tuesday afternoon to anyone who skipped the meeting. Short enough that people reply from the car.

Quick one. I'm building a rewards store for the team. You'd earn credits for closings and for finishing your checklist. Three things so I stock it right: 1. One thing under $50 you'd want 2. One thing over $250 you'd want 3. Closing gifts for your clients, yes or no? Reply here. Takes a minute. Thanks.

Why text and not email

You said agents text you back and don't open the portal. Meet them where they already answer. Who replies, and how fast, is a number we keep.

What we do with replies

Screenshot them to Danny or forward them. We tally, you decide. The tally goes on the Dec 3 call.
