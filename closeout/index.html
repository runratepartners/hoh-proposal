Two rules

What never changes

The portal is the record.If Excel and portal ever disagree, the portal wins once you've said it's trusted. Until then, Excel is the check, not the source.
Don't skip the two dates.Every other step was already happening. The two red ones are the only new habit, and they're the whole measurement.
You approve and pay every payout. We never hold or move your money or any agent's commission.

What changes next
