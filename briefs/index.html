Two of the three aren't new agents

Same three steps open on all of them

12-month closing goals. Weekly training. Orientation. Every other step is done. That's not three people stalling, that's three steps nobody owns once the paperwork's through. Fix the hand-off and this flag mostly goes away.

Jennifer and Eric are veterans

A 2014 Rookie of the Year and a 1998 license, both with zero closings under your brand in 100+ days. That's not a ramp question. Either their deals are closing somewhere the portal can't see, they're in a pipeline, or they're part-time. The call is to find out which.

Brief 1

Jennifer Essig

Signed Jul 22 · day 117 · 8 of 13 · Mountain Home · No closing in portal
Ten-plus years in the business, Rookie of the Year in 2014, top producer in Elmore County by her own bio. Zero closings under Hunter of Homes since July. Something's going on that the checklist can't see.
  • School, fingerprints, IREC, handbook, BRR, IMLS, headshot, shirt size
  • Set up HOH email address
  • Create business cards
  • Create 12-month closing goals
  • Start attending weekly training
  • Complete orientation

Open with

"Jennifer, you've been with us since July and I haven't seen a closing come through. I want to make sure that's a paperwork thing and not a you thing."

Find out

  • Is she closing deals that aren't being reported to you?
  • Is Mountain Home far enough that the Fruitland Tuesday meeting never made sense for her?
  • Does she still not have an HOH email? That's a two-minute fix on your end and it signals she's not fully in.

Leave with

Her 2027 number, in her words. And whether weekly training is realistic from Mountain Home or needs a remote option.

Brief 2

Dylan Pease

Signed Jul 31 · day 108 · 8 of 13 · Treasure Valley · No closing in portal
The one real rookie of the three. Bio is about his wife, two dogs, lake weekends, volleyball and the family farm. No production history. Day 108 with no closing is normal for a first-year agent, but day 180 isn't. This is the one to coach.
  • School, fingerprints, IREC, handbook, BRR, IMLS, headshot, shirt size
  • Set up HOH email address
  • Create business cards
  • Create 12-month closing goals
  • Start attending weekly training
  • Complete orientation

Open with

"Dylan, you're a hundred days in. Most people's first deal lands somewhere between day 90 and 180. What's in your pipeline right now?"

Find out

  • Does he have a single buyer or listing conversation going?
  • Has he been to a Tuesday meeting? The portal's meeting log doesn't show him, but it only has five meetings logged all year, so ask.
  • Is real estate the day job or the side job right now?

Leave with

One specific action for the next two weeks (an open house to sit, a farm-area mailer, a sphere list). And the goals step done, with you, on the call.

Brief 3

Eric Barnabe

Signed Aug 5 · day 103 · 10 of 13 · No closing in portal
Licensed since 1998. Buyers, sellers, investors, land, farms. Ordered business cards Oct 19, so he's still moving. Twenty-eight years in and no closing in 103 days means his pipeline is somewhere you can't see.
  • School, fingerprints, IREC, handbook, BRR, IMLS, HOH email, headshot, business cards, shirt size
  • Create 12-month closing goals
  • Start attending weekly training
  • Complete orientation

Open with

"Eric, you've been doing this longer than I have. I'm not calling to check on you, I'm calling to find out what you need from me that you haven't asked for."

Find out

  • What's pending? He almost certainly has something.
  • Does "weekly training" even apply to him, or should his version be a monthly sit-down?
  • Land and farm buyers: is that where he wants his book, and is the small-acreage niche a fit?

Leave with

His 2027 number and what he considers a good year. For a veteran, that's the whole goals step.
